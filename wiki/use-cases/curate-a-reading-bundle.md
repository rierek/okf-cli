## Purpose

Show creating and maintaining a ctx bundle.

## Audience

A user who prepares context for a recurring task or agent session.

## Required sections

### Result sentence

- Bold sentence: problem and solution in one sentence. Source: FACTS §okf ctx create.

### Set it up

- `okf ctx create <slug> <paths...>`. Source: FACTS §okf ctx create.

### Work through it

- `ctx add`, `ctx move` (order, mode), `ctx drop`; `ctx show` to review; `ctx check` to validate. Source: FACTS §okf ctx add; FACTS §okf ctx move; FACTS §okf ctx drop; FACTS §okf ctx show; FACTS §okf ctx check.

### What you get

- A bundle `okf ctx <slug>` prints in reading order with token estimates. Source: FACTS §okf ctx show.

### Limits

- The ctx write verbs write immediately; a dead entry exits 4 [vf2r]. Source: FACTS §Defect list, observations; FACTS §Defect list g.
